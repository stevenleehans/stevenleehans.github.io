Traceable adaptationSynthetic study · Model and policy evaluation
JavaScriptn8nLogistic regression
Study scope

I built the portal, workflow definitions, model inference, deterministic rules, schedule writer and audit records. Evaluated traceability, one-to-three-message weekly policies and automation using synthetic profiles. Better interaction prediction did not produce a consistent policy advantage.
