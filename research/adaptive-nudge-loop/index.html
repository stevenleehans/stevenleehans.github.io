STEP 1 / 8

A prescription goes in.

The clinician enters the patient and the prescription, and can attach the scanned original. That is the whole input.

    What this is. A research prototype built for a Monash Master of Data Science minor thesis on the technical feasibility of closed-loop adaptive messaging. The walkthrough illustrates one synthetic adaptation decision. Its example scores and counts are separate from the thesis evaluation results below.

    What it is not. All profiles, interaction histories and figures shown are synthetic, and patient names are placeholders. The system has not been evaluated with patients and makes no claim about adherence, behaviour change, or clinical benefit. Delivery, reads, visits and replies are interaction evidence only — a missing read receipt is recorded as unknown, never as unread.

    THE RESEARCH BEHIND THE WALKTHROUGH

    Can an adaptive messaging loop be built, traced and tested?

    I designed and implemented the prototype, then evaluated its technical behaviour using controlled synthetic profiles and scenarios. The study follows the full chain from prescription and approval to message scheduling, feedback and bounded frequency changes.

    01 / TRACEABILITY

    Explain every change

    Link prescriptions, approvals, deliveries, feedback, model recommendations and applied decisions. Preserve the sent history and record why a proposed change was accepted, rejected or held.

    02 / ADAPTATION

    Test the policy, not just the model

    Compare one to three messages per week using fixed schedules, preset rules and model-assisted decisions. The logistic model estimates a reply or tracked-link visit within 24 hours after confirmed delivery. This is an interaction proxy.

    03 / AUTOMATION

    Keep human approval explicit

    Separate automated scheduling and evidence handling from clinician approval. Check the implemented responsibilities and failure cases through local tests; clinician workload reduction was not measured.

    What the evaluation found

    The model predicted the synthetic interaction proxy better than a constant-rate benchmark: test Brier score 0.137 versus 0.172 (lower is better). That prediction gain did not produce a consistent efficiency advantage over two messages per week or preset rules. The model-assisted policy usually selected the minimum frequency.

    The thesis reports 269 automated tests passed and separates recommendations from applied changes. These results support bounded technical feasibility under the tested assumptions. They do not establish adherence, behaviour change, clinical benefit, real-user preference matching, or a complete live adaptive loop.

    What I built

    The portal and APIs, workflow definitions, synthetic data generator, logistic-regression training and JavaScript inference, deterministic adaptation rules, schedule writer, audit records and automated tests.
